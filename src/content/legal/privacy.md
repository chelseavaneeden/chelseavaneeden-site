This policy explains what personal information I collect through chelseavaneeden.com and my sessions, why I collect it, and the choices you have. I keep it to what I need to reply to you, teach you safely and run my business.

## 1. Who I am

Chelsea Van Eeden Pilates is run by Chelsea van Eeden, a Pilates instructor based in Lisbon, Portugal. I am responsible for your personal information (the "controller" under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR).

To ask a question about your information or use any of your rights, send me a message through the form at [chelseavaneeden.com/enquire](/enquire), or reply to any email from me.

## 2. What I collect and why

**When you send an enquiry or request a proposal.** Your name, email address and your answers. I use them to reply to you and, for organisers, to prepare a proposal. Legal basis: taking steps at your request before an agreement, and my legitimate interest in answering enquiries.

**When you book and pay.** Payments are handled by Stripe. I receive your name, email address, what you bought and whether the payment went through. I never see or store your full card details. Legal basis: performing our agreement, and my legal obligation to keep accounting records.

**When you fill in the intake form after booking.** Where you are and your time zone, your goals, the equipment you have and, if you choose to share it, information about injuries, pain, pregnancy or other health conditions. I use this only to plan safe sessions for you. Health information is a special category of data, so I only collect it with your explicit consent, which you give with the tick box on the form and can withdraw at any time.

**During our sessions.** Notes on your progress, your programme and anything you tell me that affects how we work together. Legal basis: performing our agreement and, for health information, your explicit consent.

**When you visit the website.** I use Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics tool, to count visits and clicks on buttons such as "Book your intro pack". It sets no cookies, does not build a profile of you and does not track you across other websites. I only see totals, such as how many people visited a page. Legal basis: my legitimate interest in understanding how the website is used. You can switch it off for this browser below.

**If you message me on WhatsApp.** WhatsApp is run by Meta. Using it is your choice; the website also lets you contact me by email.

## 3. Who else handles your information

I use a small number of trusted services to run my business. They only process your information on my instructions or under their own privacy policies:

- **Cloudflare**: hosts the website, sends the enquiry forms to me and runs the spam check (Turnstile) on the forms.
- **Resend**: delivers the enquiry form emails to my inbox.
- **Stripe**: processes payments. For some payment data, Stripe acts as a controller under its own privacy policy.
- **Google Workspace**: my email and calendar.
- **Umami**: website analytics, as described above.
- **My booking and video call services**: to schedule and hold our sessions.

I never sell your information or share it for advertising.

## 4. Transfers outside the EU

Some of these services may process information in the United States or elsewhere outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, they rely on safeguards recognised under the GDPR, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## 5. How long I keep it

- Enquiries that don't lead to working together: up to 2 years after our last contact.
- Client records, including session notes and health information: while we work together and up to 2 years after our last session, unless you ask me to delete them sooner.
- Payment and invoice records: for as long as Portuguese tax and accounting law requires.

## 6. Cookies

The website uses no advertising or tracking cookies. The payment page loads Stripe, and the forms may load Cloudflare's spam check. Both can set cookies that are strictly necessary for secure payments and spam protection.

## 7. Your rights

You can ask me to:

- give you a copy of the information I hold about you;
- correct anything that is wrong;
- delete your information;
- limit or object to how I use it;
- send your information to you or another provider in a portable format;
- stop using information I hold based on your consent (this doesn't affect anything I did before you withdrew it).

I will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how I handle your information, you can complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), at [cnpd.pt](https://www.cnpd.pt), or to the authority where you live.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I will update this page and the date at the top.
